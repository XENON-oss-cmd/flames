### Project Description

FLAMES is a simple web-based game that calculates the relationship between two people based on their names. Enter two names, and the website performs the classic FLAMES calculation to reveal the result.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)

People are suffering from the unbearable uncertainty of not knowing what their relationship with someone is based on their names. 😔

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)

We created FLAMES — a highly advanced relationship-analysis system™ that takes two names, performs some questionable mathematics, and confidently tells you whether you are Friends, Lovers, Affection, Marriage, Enemies, or Siblings. 🔥
